# 🪙 QuickStack
### *Restock in seconds, not minutes.* · *Nachkaufen in Sekunden statt Minuten.*

**Alt + Left-click at any vendor buys exactly the amount you set for that item.**
**Alt + Linksklick beim Händler kauft genau die Menge, die du für das Item eingestellt hast.**

---

## 🇬🇧 English

Water for the raid. Bread for the grind. Vials, thread and powders for your next craft. You buy the same things again and again, and every time it's the same dance with the quantity popup.

**QuickStack ends that.** Tell it once how many of each item you want, then just hold **Alt** and click. Every time.

### 🚀 Up and running in 30 seconds
1. **Open the menu** with the coin icon on your minimap, or type `/qs`
2. **Set your amount** per item with a slider from 1 to 20
3. **Go shopping:** at any vendor, hold **Alt** and **left-click** an item

There is no setup screen to fill in first. The first Alt-click on a new item buys it and adds it to your list automatically, ready to fine-tune. You can also drag items from your bags straight into the menu.

### 💡 Perfect for
- 🧪 **Alchemists and crafters** who restock vials, powders and thread constantly
- ⚔️ **Raiders and farmers** who always need the same water, food and consumables
- 🎒 **Anyone** who is tired of the quantity popup

### ✨ Features
- 🎚️ **A different amount for every item:** 15 water, 20 bread, 5 vials, saved automatically
- 🧭 **Draggable minimap button:** place it wherever you like along the minimap edge
- 🖼️ **Feels native:** a menu in the familiar WoW style with the classic frames, buttons and sliders
- 💰 **Safe buying:** never more than the vendor has in stock or you can afford
- 📊 **Price table at the vendor:** see at a glance whether this vendor is more expensive than another one you have visited, for example a capital where your reputation gives you a discount
- 🏷️ **Price comparison in tooltips:** known vendor prices appear in the item tooltip
- ⌨️ **Your key:** Alt, Ctrl or Shift
- 🐞 **Bug report in the game:** describe the problem and get a report you can copy and post
- 🌍 **English and German** with automatic language detection
- 🪶 **Lightweight:** no libraries, nothing running in the background

### ⌨️ Commands
| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/qs` | Opens or closes the QuickStack menu |
| `/qs alt` · `/qs ctrl` · `/qs shift` | Sets the modifier key |

### 🎮 Compatibility
Made for **Retail** and **WoW Forever**.

### 📦 Installation
**Easiest:** install with the **CurseForge app**. It puts everything in the right place for your game version.

**Manually:**
1. Download the file and unzip it
2. Copy the `QuickStack` folder into the `Interface/AddOns` folder of your game version (Retail or Forever) inside your World of Warcraft installation
3. Start the game and make sure **QuickStack** is enabled on the character selection screen

### ❓ FAQ
**How does the price comparison work?**
Every time you open a vendor, QuickStack saves the price of each item together with the vendor and the zone. At the next vendor, a table next to the vendor window shows how the prices compare to the cheapest one you know. It cannot read your reputation itself, but reputation discounts show up as lower prices at the vendors where you have them.

**What does a new item buy before I set an amount?**
A full stack, up to 20, on the first Alt-click. The item is added to your list so you can adjust it.

**The game says "Incompatible" or "Out of date".**
Tick **Load out of date AddOns** in the AddOns menu.

**Nothing happens when I click.**
Type `/reload` and check that the folder is named exactly `QuickStack` and contains `QuickStack.toc` directly.

**Where do I report bugs?**
Open the **Bug Report** tab in the menu and post the report in the comments, or create an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/sjeutes93/QuickStack/issues).

### ❓ Häufige Fragen
**Wie funktioniert der Preisvergleich?**
Jedes Mal, wenn du einen Händler öffnest, speichert QuickStack den Preis jedes Items samt Händler und Zone. Beim nächsten Händler zeigt eine Tabelle neben dem Händlerfenster, wie die Preise im Vergleich zum günstigsten bekannten stehen. Deinen Ruf kann das Addon nicht selbst auslesen, aber Rufrabatte erkennst du an den niedrigeren Preisen bei den Händlern, bei denen du sie hast.

**Was kauft ein neues Item, bevor ich eine Menge einstelle?**
Beim ersten Alt-Klick einen vollen Stack, höchstens 20. Das Item kommt in deine Liste, dort kannst du es anpassen.

**Das Spiel meldet „Inkompatibel“ oder „Veraltet“.**
Im AddOn-Menü **Veraltete AddOns laden** anhaken.

**Beim Klicken passiert nichts.**
`/reload` eingeben und prüfen, ob der Ordner exakt `QuickStack` heißt und direkt `QuickStack.toc` enthält.

**Wo melde ich Fehler?**
Reiter **Bug melden** im Menü öffnen und den Bericht in die Kommentare posten oder ein Issue auf [GitHub](https://github.com/sjeutes93/QuickStack/issues) erstellen.
